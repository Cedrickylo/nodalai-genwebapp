    Frequently Asked Questions

    How does Nodal AI generate quizzes?

    It processes text from your uploaded PDFs or Word documents locally and uses on-device smart rules alongside secure cloud processing to instantly construct multiple-choice, identification, or enumeration questions.

    Can I use Nodal AI completely offline?

    Yes! Once the app loads on your device, you can review your entire local history, customize options, and play any previously generated quiz without an active internet connection. Generating new quizzes requires network access.

    Where is my quiz history stored?

    Your history is saved locally in your browser's secure cache database. If you log in with your Puter account, your records will automatically synchronize seamlessly across all your devices.

    Is there a daily quiz generation limit?

    To prevent abuse, there is a sliding rate limit of 5 quizzes every 3 hours per user session. You can monitor your current cooldown status inside your Account tab.

    About

    Purpose

    Nodal AI Quiz Generator is a lightweight tool to convert learning materials into practice quizzes quickly. It's designed for educators and learners to create formative assessments from documents.

    Creator

    Created by the Nodal AI team (or contributor). For inquiries, contact the project owner listed in the repository.

    Quick Steps

    1. Select or upload documents using "Select Documents".
    2. Adjust quiz settings (number of questions, difficulty, timing) in "Customize Quiz Settings".
    3. Click "Generate Quiz" to create questions from your documents.
    4. Take the quiz, review results, and export or save to the cloud.

    What this site does

    It analyzes uploaded documents and generates assessment-style questions (multiple choice, identification, enumeration, or a mix) using on-device logic and cloud helpers. You can save quizzes to your Puter account or export them locally.

    Limitations & Notes

    • Quality of questions depends on the clarity and structure of the source document.
    • Processing large or scanned PDFs may be slower and may not extract text perfectly.
    • There is a rate limit on quiz generation to prevent abuse (see account modal).
    • Some features rely on Puter cloud services; offline behavior is limited.

    Troubleshooting

    If uploads fail or generation stalls, try re-uploading a simpler file, check your internet connection for cloud sync features, or refresh the page.

    Services & Credits

    This project uses the following third-party services and libraries:

    • Puter.js — cloud sync & user accounts (credits apply to some operations).
    • mammoth.js — Word document parsing.
    • pdf.js — PDF text extraction.
    • tone.js — audio feedback (optional).
    • crypto-js and lz-string — for local data handling and compression.

    Privacy

    Uploaded documents may be temporarily processed by Puter services when cloud features are enabled. Please avoid uploading sensitive personal data.
