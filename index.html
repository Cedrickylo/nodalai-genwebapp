    Frequently Asked Questions

    How does Nodal AI generate quizzes?

    It processes text from your uploaded PDFs or Word documents locally and uses on-device smart rules alongside secure cloud processing to instantly construct multiple-choice, identification, or enumeration questions.

    Can I use Nodal AI offline? How does offline retention work?

    Yes! Quizzes less than 1 week old are automatically cached on your device. For any quiz, open Quiz Options and tap "Make Available Offline" to download the quiz and its full statistics for 1 week of offline use. You can view storage usage and renew or remove quizzes anytime in the Offline Downloads tab.

    How do Quiz Statistics & Past Test Review work?

    Every completed quiz session is saved as a separate take. In Quiz Statistics, you can inspect your progress over time (average score, best score, retake counts) and click "Review" on any past take to inspect all your submitted answers alongside correct answers and explanations.

    How does Cloud Sync work across multiple devices?

    When you log in with your Puter account, your quiz library, settings, and retake history synchronize across all your devices. If you complete quizzes or edit settings on different devices while offline, the cloud sync automatically merges and re-indexes all retakes chronologically and resolves settings edits cleanly.

    Is there a daily quiz generation limit?

    To ensure fair usage, there is a sliding rate limit of 5 quizzes every 3 hours per user session. You can monitor your current cooldown status inside your Account tab.

    About

    Purpose

    Nodal AI Quiz Generator is a lightweight tool to convert learning materials into practice quizzes quickly. It's designed for educators and learners to create formative assessments from documents.

    Developer & Creator

    Developed and created by John Cedrick Siason (@cedrickylo). Designed for learners and educators to easily convert learning materials into practice quizzes and study aids.

    Quick Steps

    1. Select or upload documents using "Select Documents".
    2. Adjust quiz settings (question count, difficulty, timer mode) in "Customize Quiz Settings".
    3. Click "Generate Quiz" to create questions from your documents.
    4. Take the quiz, track your attempts, and review results with detailed answer keys.
    5. Track score trends in Quiz Statistics and download quizzes for 1 week of offline practice.

    What this site does

    Nodal AI analyzes uploaded documents and builds tailored interactive assessments. It supports installable PWA usage across mobile and desktop, multi-device cloud syncing with Puter, dedicated Quiz Statistics with past test review, and intelligent 1-week offline downloads.

    Limitations & Notes

    • Quality of questions depends on the clarity and structure of the source document.
    • Processing large or scanned PDFs may be slower and may not extract text perfectly.
    • There is a rate limit on quiz generation to prevent abuse (see account modal).
    • Offline mode lets you take and review any downloaded or recent quiz without network connectivity; generating new quizzes requires an active internet connection.

    Troubleshooting

    If uploads fail or generation stalls, try re-uploading a simpler file, check your internet connection for cloud sync features, or refresh the page.

    Services & Credits

    This project uses the following third-party services and libraries:

    • Puter.js — cloud sync & user accounts (credits apply to some operations).
    • mammoth.js — Word document parsing.
    • pdf.js — PDF text extraction.
    • tone.js — audio feedback (optional).
    • crypto-js and lz-string — for local data handling and compression.

    Privacy

    Uploaded documents may be temporarily processed by Puter services when cloud features are enabled. Please avoid uploading sensitive personal data.
