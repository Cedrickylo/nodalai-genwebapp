PRIVACY & DATA DISCLOSURE

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Version 2.4

Executive Privacy Guarantee:

Nodal AI operates under a Client-First, Zero-Knowledge architectural philosophy. We do not sell your personal data, we do not deploy advertising or tracking pixels, and your uploaded lecture slides, notes, and PDF textbooks are extracted directly in your browser without ever being uploaded to permanent remote database servers.

1. Where Your Data Goes & Processing Architecture

Nodal AI processes data across clearly defined, segregated layers:

  • In-Browser Document Extraction: Files you supply (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT) are read directly inside browser web workers using client libraries (Mozilla PDF.js, Mammoth.js, JSZip). The source files reside exclusively in your computer's RAM during active use and are never sent to external servers.
  • Google Gemini 3.5 API: When generating questions, extracted text is formatted into an ephemeral study prompt and sent via encrypted HTTPS to Google Gemini. Under developer API service terms, prompt payloads are processed transiently and are not retained or utilized to train public foundation models.
  • Client-Side Storage: Your quiz history, score cards, and answer sheets are saved locally on your device via HTML5 IndexedDB and LocalStorage, encrypted using client-side AES-GCM.
  • Optional Puter.com Cloud Sync: If you explicitly sign in with Puter, your quiz libraries are synced directly to your sandboxed Puter user directory (/app/nodal_ai/). Nodal AI maintains no centralized database holding user credentials.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of young learners is paramount. Nodal AI strictly adheres to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • Users Under the Age of 13: Must obtain verifiable parental or legal guardian consent prior to creating a cloud-synced account or utilizing AI generation features.
  • Parental Consent Protocol: Parents or guardians may contact our compliance team to review, modify, or permanently delete any information submitted by their children.
  • Users Aged 13–17: May utilize the application for educational purposes with parental or educational institution awareness of our Terms and Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Nodal AI does not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or behavioral analytics software. Our storage is limited to strictly necessary preferences (audio mute status, theme, offline cache keys). See our full Cookie Policy for detailed breakdowns.

5. Your Rights: Data Deletion & Portability (GDPR / CCPA)

Regardless of your geographic location, you retain full ownership of your study materials:

  • Instant Local Deletion: You can completely destroy all local quizzes, history, and encryption keys with 1 click using the "Wipe Local Device Data" button on the homepage or through browser settings.
  • Cloud Sync Deletion Request: If you utilized cloud synchronization and wish to request full account erasure, email your request to our compliance administrator: jc.siason08@gmail.com. Requests are completed within 48 business hours.
  • Data Portability: You can export all your quiz sets at any time in standardized JSON or encrypted .nodal formats.

6. Security Measures

We employ multi-layer security protections including HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS with 2-year preload), Content Security Policy (CSP), rate limiting, prompt injection defenses, input sanitization, and client-side AES-GCM encryption.
