From 80-page scientific journal articles to dense medical textbook chapters, Mozilla PDF.js extracts text line-by-line within browser canvas workers with zero server roundtrips.

  • Cleans headers, footers, and page numbers
  • Token budgeting optimized for Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite

Lecture presentations often contain crucial bulleted exam concepts. JSZip unpacks the XML structures of `.pptx` presentations directly in memory, harvesting every slide bullet.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our privacy model, offline capabilities, and architecture.

How does Nodal AI guarantee document privacy?
When you drop a PDF, PowerPoint, or Word file into Nodal AI, parsing executes 100% locally in your browser memory using PDF.js and Mammoth.js. Your source document is never transmitted to, uploaded to, or stored on external database servers. Only the constructed prompt text is sent ephemerally to the AI model to generate question structures.
Can I use Nodal AI completely offline?
Yes! Nodal AI is an installable Progressive Web App (PWA). All generated quizzes, scoring history, answer keys, and audio feedback are cached in your device's IndexedDB for 7 days. You can practice and review previously generated quizzes on flights, subways, or during internet disruptions with zero connectivity.
What AI engine powers Nodal AI?
Nodal AI uses Google Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite (with automatic fallback to Gemini 3.5 Flash and Gemini 2.5). The engine combines high reasoning speed with robust prompt validation and self-healing JSON correction loops to ensure reliable, high-yield practice questions.
How do I transfer quizzes between my laptop and mobile phone?
You have two privacy-respecting options: (1) Export an encrypted .nodal file with AES-GCM encryption and import it on another device, or (2) Sign in with Puter.com to sync your library seamlessly to your private cloud storage sandbox.
Is Nodal AI really free? What's the catch?
There is no catch. Nodal AI is a free, open-source educational project licensed under the MIT License. We do not sell your data, run third-party advertising trackers, or charge subscription fees.
🍪 Privacy & Storage Transparency Zero Ad Trackers

Nodal AI uses strictly necessary local browser storage (IndexedDB and LocalStorage) to keep your quizzes available offline and encrypt your data. We do not use third-party advertising or behavioral tracking cookies.
