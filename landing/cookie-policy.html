STORAGE TRANSPARENCY

Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Version 2.4

1. Overview: No Advertising Trackers

Nodal AI does not partner with advertising networks, third-party data brokers, or behavioral analytics providers. We do not place cookies for cross-site tracking or profiling.

2. What Storage Mechanisms We Use

To make Nodal AI functional as an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) with instant response times, we utilize standard HTML5 browser storage technologies:

Key / Store Name Type Purpose Duration
nodal_cookie_consent LocalStorage Remembers your consent preference so the banner does not repeat Persistent
nodal_audio_muted LocalStorage Stores your preference for synthesizer audio chime feedback Persistent
nodal_age_verified LocalStorage Records that student/parental COPPA age check was confirmed Persistent
nodal_ai_db IndexedDB Stores generated quizzes, score history, and question cards for 1-week offline retention Up to 7 days / User Cleared
Puter Auth Session Secure Cookie / Storage Used strictly if you sign in to Puter.com to sync your private cloud storage Session / User Logout

3. How to Manage and Delete Your Storage

Because all quiz data is stored client-side, you have total control over it:

  • In Nodal AI: Click "Wipe Local Device Data" in the Data Deletion Request modal to immediately purge all IndexedDB and LocalStorage items.
  • In Your Browser: Open Developer Tools (F12) → Application → Clear Storage, or clear browser cookies and site data for the domain.

4. Updates to this Policy

Any updates to our storage practices will be reflected in this document and announced via release notes.
